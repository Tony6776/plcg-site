What We Build

Four specialist construction services, all delivered to NDIS Practice Standards and current SDA Design rules.

🏠

SDA New Build

Ground-up construction of Specialist Disability Accommodation dwellings to one of the four NDIS SDA design categories: Improved Liveability, Robust, Fully Accessible or High Physical Support.

🔧

Apartment SDA Conversion

Refurbishment of existing apartments to SDA specification. Suitable for investor-owned units in metro locations where land development is impractical.

📐

Compliance & Design

Working with NDIS-accredited SDA Assessors and Building Surveyors, we deliver projects with full SDA Design Certification ready for NDIA enrolment.

🤝

Project Management

End-to-end coordination — from valuations and feasibility through subcontractor management, finishes, and handover to the SDA Provider for tenant placement.

📊

Investor-Funded Builds

Build-to-spec delivery for SDA investors via SDA Capital + InvestFirst pathways. Transparent project accounting, milestone-based progress payments, NDIA enrolment support.

🏗️

POP Pathway Builds

Construction support for Homelander Participant Ownership Program pathways — Deposit Ready, Rent-to-Buy, and Equity Share / Government Co-Ownership.

About PLCG

PLCG (Property · Land · Construction Group) is the construction arm of the Homelander group. We build the SDA properties that Homelander operates and that SDA Capital and InvestFirst clients invest in.

Group Position

One of seven brands in the Homelander group, alongside the registered SDA provider, the participant ownership program, the channel partner network, and the investor brands.

Compliance Posture

Operating under NSW Home Building Act 1989 builders licence. HBCF insurance for residential work over A$20,000. 6-year statutory warranty applied. NDIS Q&S compliance via parent provider.

Quality Standard

Every build meets current NDIS SDA Design Standard. Independent SDA Assessor certification on completion. Building Surveyor sign-off at every milestone.
